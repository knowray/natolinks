Capability Briefs

The systems that turn promises into force

A defence budget is only an input. NATOlinks follows the enabling capabilities that allow national forces to see, move, survive, communicate, supply and fight together.

Protect

Integrated air & missile defence

Europe’s challenge is layered coverage: sensors, command networks, interceptors, launchers, trained crews, stockpiles and the ability to coordinate across borders. A delivery announcement is not coverage; integration and sustained ammunition matter.

Monitor: sensor networks, interceptor production, programme interoperability, fielding dates and protection of critical infrastructure.

See

ISR, space & surveillance

Intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance connects every other capability. The issue is not only platforms; it is data fusion, secure communications, national release rules, drones, maritime awareness and the ability to produce a shared picture fast.

Monitor: airborne surveillance, satellite services, uncrewed systems, data networks and information-sharing arrangements.

Reach

Deep strike & long-range fires

Long-range precision fires shape deterrence, but they also impose demanding requirements: targeting, stockpiles, training, political authorization and industrial depth. This is one of the areas where European replacement of U.S. enablers is hardest.

Monitor: procurement, ammunition, targeting support, range, survivability and interoperability.

Move

Military mobility & logistics

Forces must cross ports, railheads, bridges, borders and airfields before they can be relevant. Logistics is the alliance’s circulatory system: fuel, maintenance, medical support, host-nation arrangements and resilient routes.

Monitor: transport corridors, port capacity, bridge/rail upgrades, permits, fuel, pre-positioning and sustainment exercises.

Sustain

Munitions & production depth

Ukraine has made production rate a strategic variable. Orders must become lines, inputs, labour, testing, storage and delivered rounds. The key question is not a press release announcing expansion, but when output arrives and how long it can be sustained.

Monitor: multi-year contracts, propellant/explosives supply, production capacity, delivery batches and stockpile policy.

Adapt

Drones & counter-drone

Uncrewed systems have shortened the cycle between battlefield adaptation and industrial production. This field rewards rapid testing, cheap scale, software resilience and countermeasures—not simply buying exquisite platforms.

Monitor: doctrine, electronic warfare, procurement speed, industrial ecosystem, testing ranges and counter-UAS coverage.

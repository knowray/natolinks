NATO 3.0 Transition Monitor

From promise to usable force: a transparent method for tracking Europe’s conventional-defence transition.

Method

One record, seven stages

Commitment → budget → programme → supplier → production → fielded capability → remaining gap.

The country monitor below includes all 32 NATO allies, with individual research priorities and funding-source links. Overall assessments require dated evidence and a published rationale.

Do not score secrets

“Alliance reliability” should use public evidence—deployments, exercises, hosting arrangements, parliamentary constraints, public policy and force contributions—not claims about classified access agreements.
